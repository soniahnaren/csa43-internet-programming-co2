A Weekend in Kodaikanal

Posted on 27 Oct 2026 by Admin

Kodaikanal is a calm hill town known for its misty mornings, lakes and pine forests. We started early to catch the sunrise from Coaker's Walk.

The best way to explore is on foot. Carry a light jacket, because the weather changes quickly during the day.
